## The four roles

### Admin (`ADMIN`)

Lands on **Transakcje** (`/`) after login.

**Can:**

- Everything Właściciel can.
- Also the two diagnostic routes `/api/test-email` and `/api/test-push`. These are the only things Właściciel can't do.

**Can't:**

- Deactivate, trash or delete his own account, or the last live Właściciel/Admin.
- Book an amount on a „Zakończona” investment until it is reopened.

### Właściciel (`OWNER`)

Lands on **Transakcje** (`/`) after login.

**Can:**

- Everything management can, plus everything in the Admin/Właściciel column of the comparison below: premia, kasa główna, marża, reopening a „Zakończona” investment, renaming szablony, recipient lists, „Dostawcy i ceny”, any account and any kasa.

**Can't:**

- The two Admin-only test routes.
- Same self-protection rules as Admin.

### Manager (`MANAGER`)

Lands on **Transakcje** (`/`) after login. Same nav as Właściciel.

**Can:**

- Run the business day to day: book transakcje (except premia), settle wypłaty, edit kosztorysy and szablony, katalog, flota, sprzęt, zgłoszenia, Kosz.
- Manage Pracownik accounts and his own; manage his own kasy and Pracownik kasy.

**Can't:**

- See kasa główna or marża, book premia, reopen a „Zakończona” investment, rename a szablon.
- Touch Admin, Właściciel or other Manager accounts; give anyone a role other than Pracownik.
- Edit or cancel another person's transakcja (except robocizna, and cancelling a wypłata the worker rejected).

### Pracownik (`EMPLOYEE`)

Lands on **his own** `/pracownicy/{id}`. Any management page sends him back there.

**Can:**

- His own worker page: send a wydatek (as a draft), open his report link, confirm or reject his wypłaty, see his kasy, transakcje and history.
- Change his own e-mail, password and language; push notifications; install the app.

**Can't:**

- Book any transakcja, open any other page, see the nav, Kosz, Admin, „Manual Wykończymy” or the kosztorys editor.
- Use the Payload REST API for anything but files and his own login.

## How a check runs

1. **Proxy** — Only checks that a `payload-token` cookie exists. No role check. Share links and legal pages pass without one. (`proxy.ts:3-30`)
2. **Shell layout** — Verifies the session. A dead or stale cookie goes to `/zaloguj`. (`(frontend)/layout.tsx:67`)
3. **Page guard** — The real role gate for pages. Refused Pracownik ends on his own worker page. (`require-auth.ts, require-management-page.ts`)
4. **Action wrapper** — Every server action runs through one. This is where most rules live. (`lib/actions/run-action.ts`)
5. **Payload hooks** — Run for actions and REST alike. Lock, trash and transfer-shape rules. No role rules, except reopening a „Zakończona” investment. (`collections/hooks/*`)

The session check also refuses an account that is inactive, trashed, or whose role changed since login (`lib/db/user-sessions.ts:17-35`). Payload collection `access` blocks apply only to REST; server actions skip them (`overrideAccess` defaults to true), so the wrapper and the handler are the whole gate there.

| Wrapper            | Gate                                                                    |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `protectedAction`  | Management only. No other check.                                        |
| `sessionAction`    | Every role. The handler must limit the write to the caller.             |
| `ownerOnlyAction`  | Admin and Właściciel. Manager is refused with a message.                |
| `investmentAction` | Management, and refuses an investment in Kosz or „Zakończona”.          |
| `templateAction`   | Management, and refuses a szablon in Kosz.                              |
| `tokenAction`      | No login. The share token decides, and the worker comes from the token. |

## Pracownik and people without an account

### Signed in as Pracownik

- Own `/pracownicy/{id}` only. Another worker's id gives 404.
- „Wydatek”: sends paragony as a zgłoszenie wydatków to an investment where he has an etap, from his own live kasa. Management books it.
- „Zgłoś pracę”: his personal report link.
- „Wypłaty do potwierdzenia”: „Potwierdzam” or „Odrzuć” with a reason.
- Delete his own pending zgłoszenie wydatków.
- His kasy, held sprzęt, transakcje, report history (no links to management pages).
- „Zmień e-mail lub hasło” (current password checked), „Domyślny język”, push, install.
- Shell: his name, theme, „Odśwież dane”, UI scale, push, „Wyloguj”. No section links.
- Can upload files (any signed-in role can).

### Holding a link, no login

- **Investor link** `/k/[token]`: read-only kosztorys with version history, shaped by „Ustawienia podglądu inwestora”. 404 when revoked or the investment is in Kosz. Still shows a „Zakończona” or rejected offer.
- **Worker report link** `/z/…/[token]`: his scope of the rozpiska in his language. He can send a zgłoszenie prac without logging in. Shows a notice when the investment is closed or in Kosz, his account is off, or no etap is open.
- **Reset-password link**: sets a new password.
- **Anyone at all**: login pages, legal pages, „Odśwież dane” (cache purge), and the Payload file list `GET /api/media` (see open questions).

A signed-in manager opening a share link sees the same anonymous view.
