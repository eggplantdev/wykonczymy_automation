# EX-1019 — round-trip audit

Measured on a local `next start` against the **preview (staging) DB** with a per-statement SQL log
(`src/instrumentation.ts`, worktree-only). Laptop → Neon is ~35 ms per round trip, Vercel → Neon
~1–2 ms, so **counts and waterfall depth are the headline; local ms are a hint, ×~20 inflated**.
Harness: `perf-audit/measure.mjs` (worktree-only) — 1 cold + 3 warm loads per page.

Columns: `sql` = statements per load, `depth` = longest chain of sequential DB round trips.

## Candidates

Status: `open` → `measured` → `fixed` / `filed EX-…` / `dropped (reason)` / `closed EX-… (reason)`. Bar for anything not clearly free (owner, 2026-10-15): a real gain, low risk, **no stale-data risk** — stale data is worse than evicting too often.

across restarts), first load after a restart, pages in baseline order. Cold milliseconds are noise
